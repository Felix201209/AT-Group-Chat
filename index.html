Capabilities

Built for local AI collaboration.

Chat is the front door. Work items, reviews, decisions, and event logs keep the collaboration real.

Manager-Controlled Team

One manager decides who speaks next. Specialists can see the transcript, but they do not free-run into loops.

Shared Transcript

Every role has isolated memory plus access to team context, so reviews and decisions stay grounded.

Work Items

Create issues, proposals, reviews, decisions, and dispatch a work item directly back to the manager.
